- **Read first.** The proposal's three facts hold in the source: `resume!` and `coroutine-wake!` take no lock; `scheduler-turn!` polls `'once` with nothing ready; the facade exports no libuv name. libuv 1.52.1's `uv_run` (`src/unix/core.c`, `src/win/core.c`), in `'once` and `'nowait`, polls, running the async callbacks, before it runs the timers, which contradicts the proposal's suggestion for its row 9.
