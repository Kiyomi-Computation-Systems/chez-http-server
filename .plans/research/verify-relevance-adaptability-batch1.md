material (keep-alive, pipelining, framing precedence, close, upgrade, limits, timeouts,
smuggling) that we can turn into byte streams / scripted client sessions to replay — or
is it parser-only (redundant with the llhttp corpus chez-httparse already conforms to),
client-only, or HTTP/2-only? Default `refuted=true` applies to the latter three classes.
